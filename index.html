    Bubble Shooter

    Bubble Shooter is a simple and addictive puzzle game where the objective is to clear the screen by matching three or more bubbles of the same color.The gameplay is straightforward: aim, shoot, and connect bubbles to make them pop. Despite its simplicity, the game requires planning and accuracy, especially as the screen fills up and available shots become limited.Its easy-to-learn mechanics and relaxing pace are the main reasons it has remained popular for years, offering a quick and engaging way to pass time without complex rules or controls.

    How to Play Bubble Shooter

    In Bubble Shooter, the objective is to score as many points as possible by popping bubbles. You do this by matching at least three bubbles of the same color.

    Basic Gameplay

    • Aim using the direction arrow at the bottom center
    • Shoot bubbles toward matching colors
    • Match 3 or more bubbles to pop them
    • New bubbles appear continuously until you lose

    You cannot choose the bubble color, it's random so you must adapt your shots and plan ahead.

    Controls

    • Move mouse – Aim your shot 
    • Left click – Shoot the bubble

    The next bubble is shown in advance, allowing you to prepare your move.

    Game Mechanics

    • Chain reactions – If you break a group, any disconnected bubbles also fall and pop
    • Score system – More bubbles popped at once = higher points
    • Rising difficulty – A new row appears after every 6 shots
    • Game over – If bubbles reach the bottom, the game ends

    Strategy Tips

    • Use walls to bounce shots into hard-to-reach areas
    • Target weak connection points to trigger chain drops
    • Watch the next bubble to plan ahead
    • Avoid random shooting—every shot should have a purpose

    Release Date

    The modern online version of Bubble Shooter was released in September 2018.

    Developer

    Originally developed by Absolutist, with updated versions by Ilyon.

    Platforms

    • Web browser (desktop and mobile)
      • Android
      • iOS

    FAQ

    Can You Win at Bubble Shooter?

    Yes. In Bubble Shooter, you win by clearing all bubbles in a level. However, many versions are endless or level-based, so “winning” depends on the mode you’re playing.

    Who Created Bubble Shooter?

    The game was originally developed by Absolutist and later modernized by Ilyon.

    How Do You Play Bubble Shooter?

    Aim and shoot bubbles to match three or more of the same color:

    •  Desktop – Move mouse to aim, click to shoot
    •  Mobile – Tap to shoot

    How Many Levels Are in Bubble Shooter?

    Many versions include 3,000+ levels, though the exact number depends on the platform and version.
